# 02 — First-Line Triage Flow

## Standard sequence

1. **Acknowledge** — confirm receipt and ownership of the ticket
2. **Clarify** — what exactly is broken / needed, who is affected, since when
3. **Categorise** — incident vs request vs other; correct category and subcategory
4. **Prioritise** — impact + urgency (see priority note)
5. **Initial diagnosis** — known error? recent change? obvious misconfiguration?
6. **Resolve or escalate** — fix within L1 scope or hand off cleanly
7. **Document** — what was tried, what was found, next action, customer update

## Minimum information before escalating

- Clear description of the issue and business impact
- Steps already performed and their results
- Relevant identifiers (user, device, ticket history, error messages)
- Time window and whether the issue is intermittent or constant

## Avoid

- Escalating with only "user says it doesn't work"
- Changing priority without recording why
- Closing without confirming with the user when the process requires it
