# 05 — Customer Communication

## Principles

- Acknowledge quickly
- Use plain language
- Set expectations on next steps and timing when possible
- Update when status changes, not only when resolved
- Confirm resolution with the user when required by process

## Useful patterns

- "I have received your request and I am looking into it."
- "Here is what I have checked so far…"
- "I am escalating this to [team] because [reason]. You will receive updates from them / via this ticket."
- "Please confirm whether the issue is resolved on your side."

## Under pressure

- Stay factual; avoid blame
- Do not promise timelines you cannot control
- Escalate early if major-incident criteria are approaching
- Keep the ticket as the source of truth so handoffs stay clean

## Language

Support in Greek and English requires the same structure: clear ownership, clear next action, no ambiguity about who is doing what next.
