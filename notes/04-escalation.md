# 04 — Escalation

## When to escalate

- Issue is outside L1 technical scope or access rights
- Known error exists and is owned by another team
- SLA risk is high and L1 cannot resolve in time
- Security or major-incident criteria are met

## Functional vs hierarchical

- **Functional** — to a team with the required skills / access (e.g. network, app support)
- **Hierarchical** — to a manager when authority, resources or major-incident declaration is needed

## Clean escalation checklist

- Ticket type and priority are correct
- Description is clear and current
- Actions already taken are listed
- Supporting data attached (logs, screenshots, error codes)
- Customer has been informed that the ticket is moving to another team

## After escalation

- Monitor for updates if process requires L1 to stay involved
- Do not leave the customer without a status when ownership changes
