# 03 — Priority, Urgency and Impact

## Definitions (practical)

- **Impact** — how many users / how critical the service is
- **Urgency** — how quickly a resolution is needed
- **Priority** — combination of impact and urgency (drives SLA)

## Typical matrix logic

| | Low urgency | High urgency |
|--|-------------|--------------|
| **High impact** | Medium / High | Critical |
| **Low impact** | Low | Medium |

Exact labels depend on the organisation’s priority matrix.

## First-line responsibility

- Set priority using the published matrix, not personal preference
- Document the reason when priority is raised or lowered
- Re-evaluate if new information changes impact or urgency

## Common mistakes

- Setting everything to high "to get attention"
- Ignoring VIP or critical-service flags defined by the organisation
- Not updating priority when scope expands (e.g. one user → whole team)
